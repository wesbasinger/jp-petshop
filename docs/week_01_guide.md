# Week 1 Field Guide: Opening the Haven

Use a paper ledger for each calculation, then enter only your final answer into the program.

## Operations

For repeated equal costs, multiply the unit cost by the number of units:

`total cost = number of units x cost per unit`

## Finance

Monthly compound interest uses:

`A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt)`

Use a decimal for the annual rate, `n = 12` for monthly compounding, and `t` in years.

## Biology

Continuous growth uses:

`P(t) = P_0 x e^(rt)`

Here, `r` is the growth rate per week and `t` is the number of weeks.

## Market Strategy

For a quadratic revenue function `R(p) = ap^2 + bp + c`, the vertex occurs at:

`p = -b / (2a)`

When `a` is negative, the vertex gives the maximum revenue.
